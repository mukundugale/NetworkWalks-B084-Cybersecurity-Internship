## 📈 Learning Approach

I follow a structured approach to practical cybersecurity work:

1. **Understand the objective** — identify the task requirements and scope.
2. **Prepare the environment** — verify the lab, connectivity, and required tools.
3. **Perform reconnaissance** — collect relevant information within the authorized scope.
4. **Analyze results** — interpret evidence and investigate potential security issues.
5. **Validate findings** — distinguish confirmed results from assumptions.
6. **Document the work** — record commands, results, troubleshooting, and lessons learned.
7. **Review and improve** — identify knowledge gaps and prepare for the next assignment.

My focus is on understanding the reasoning behind each step rather than simply copying commands.
